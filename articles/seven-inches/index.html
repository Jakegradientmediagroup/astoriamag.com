The 7 Inches Your Sex Life Might Be Missing

By Priya Holloway · 8 min read · Marriage & Intimacy

There are certain products you immediately understand.

A vibrator provides stimulation. Lingerie creates anticipation. A candle sets the mood.

An intimacy pillow is harder to explain without making it sound either vaguely orthopedic or much more complicated than it actually is.

The Prim, a seven-inch curved pillow from sexual wellness brand Tabu, looks like something you would place thoughtfully at the end of a beautifully made bed. Its linen cover is understated, its shape is discreet, and nothing about it announces that its real purpose is to make sex significantly more interesting.

But that is exactly what it does.

The Prim elevates a woman's hips and changes how her body is positioned during sex. The difference sounds subtle until you experience it. Positions you have done dozens of times suddenly create different contact, deeper sensation, and a level of access that is difficult to replicate by shoving a normal pillow underneath you.

My husband and I assumed it would be a fun accessory.

We did not expect it to change which positions we preferred.

The Difference Is in the Positioning

The first time I placed the Prim underneath my hips, the effect was immediate.

My body was lifted into a position that felt more open, more exposed, and noticeably more receptive. My husband had a clearer view of me and more freedom to control his depth and movement. I could relax into the position without losing the elevation that was creating the sensation in the first place.

It made a familiar position feel less familiar.

The pleasure felt more concentrated. The contact was more consistent. And because my hips stayed lifted, my husband could maintain the exact positioning that worked instead of losing it every time one of us moved.

There was also something undeniably sexy about the posture itself.

The Prim does not merely place a pillow beneath your body. It presents your body differently. Your hips are elevated, your back naturally arches, and your husband is given a perspective he normally does not have.

That visual element should not be underestimated.

Women are often told that great sex begins with feeling confident in their bodies. But confidence does not always need to come before the experience. Sometimes, being placed in a position where you feel especially feminine, desirable, and wanted creates the confidence for you.

It Makes Familiar Positions Feel New

Most couples have a small number of positions they return to regularly.

That is not necessarily a problem. Certain positions become favorites because they work. But familiarity can eventually make even good sex feel predictable.

The Prim adds novelty without requiring you to perform a new personality in the bedroom.

You are not memorizing elaborate instructions or attempting something you saw online that looks significantly less graceful in real life. You are taking positions you already enjoy and changing the way your bodies meet within them.

During missionary, the elevation made penetration feel deeper and gave my husband better access to my clitoris. Small changes in the placement of my legs produced noticeably different sensations, which made the position feel much more versatile than it usually does.

When I was on top, the Prim created a raised surface that allowed me to move against him with more control. I could shift the pressure, change the rhythm, and find the positioning I wanted without constantly moving both of our bodies.

From behind, it allowed me to stay lifted and angled in a way that felt both more intense and more visually exciting.

That is where the product becomes much more than a supportive pillow.

It expands what you can do with the positions you already know.

It Creates a Different Kind of Confidence

There is a tendency to discuss female pleasure as though women need to retreat inward to experience it.

Close your eyes. Clear your mind. Stop overthinking. Focus on the sensation.

But sometimes the most exciting thing a woman can feel during sex is not inward at all. It is the awareness that her husband is completely captivated by her.

The Prim enhances that feeling.

The positioning makes your body more visible. It naturally emphasizes your hips and creates an openness that can feel vulnerable in the best possible way. It allows you to see his reaction while also feeling the physical effect of the position.

Instead of feeling like an accessory added to sex, the pillow makes your body feel like the focal point of it.

That shift is subtle, but powerful.

It is difficult to feel self-conscious when the person in front of you is clearly enjoying the view.

It Does What a Bed Pillow Cannot

Naturally, the first objection is that you could simply use a pillow you already own.

You can try.

A regular pillow may work for the first two seconds. Then it compresses beneath your body, folds awkwardly, or slides backward across the mattress. If you stack several together, they tend to separate at the exact moment you would prefer not to stop and rebuild a pillow arrangement.

The Prim stays where it is supposed to stay.

Its foam is firm enough to preserve the elevation while still feeling soft against the body. You can move, shift positions, or place more weight on it without immediately flattening it.

That stability is what allows the positioning to become part of the experience rather than an interruption to it.

And because the Prim looks like a sophisticated linen pillow, it can remain on the bed instead of being hidden in a drawer or closet. It is available the moment you want it, which makes it far more likely to become part of your actual sex life rather than an accessory you remember twice a year.

My only practical criticism is that anything designed for sex should ideally include a waterproof protective layer beneath its removable cover. The linen cover can be washed, but an additional barrier would make the product feel even more thoughtfully designed.

Is It Really Worth $195?

At $195, The Prim is not an impulse purchase.

If you think of it strictly as a pillow, the price can feel difficult to justify. If you think of it as something designed to improve an experience you return to hundreds (or thousands) of times over the course of a relationship, it begins to feel reasonable.

Couples can easily spend the same amount on one dinner, a night away, or lingerie that may only come out occasionally. The Prim is less dramatic, but potentially more useful. It does not create a single memorable evening; it quietly changes the experience every time you use it.

That does not mean everyone needs one. If you are already perfectly happy with the positions and sensations you tend to return to, it may feel like an unnecessary luxury.

But if you have ever wondered whether a small change in positioning could make familiar sex feel better, the Prim offers a very polished answer. It is well made, discreet enough to leave on the bed, and versatile enough that it does not feel tied to one particular use.

The price still gave me pause. What ultimately made it feel worthwhile was not that it transformed our sex life overnight, but that we kept reaching for it.

After a while, it stopped feeling like an accessory and started feeling like part of the bed.

It Is Not Just for Couples Trying to Fix Something

The Prim is often positioned as a solution for comfort, mobility, or physical limitations. It can certainly help in those situations.

But framing it primarily that way misses its broader appeal.

You do not need to be experiencing pain. You do not need to be recovering from pregnancy. You do not need to have a problem with your sex life.

You can simply want sex to feel more intense.

You can want to explore how different positioning affects your pleasure. You can want more options without introducing something intimidating or unfamiliar into your bedroom. You can want a product that makes your husband look at you as though he has never seen you in quite that way before.

The Prim is not only about making difficult positions easier.

It is about making good positions better.

The Verdict

I expected the Prim to feel like an optional accessory: enjoyable, attractive, but ultimately unnecessary.

Instead, it revealed how much of sexual pleasure is influenced by something most of us rarely think about.

Positioning.

A few inches of elevation changed the depth, the contact, the view, and the way both of us moved. It made familiar positions feel fresh without making the experience feel artificial.

Most importantly, it did not feel like a product designed to correct a deficiency.

It felt indulgent.

The kind of indulgence that becomes part of your routine because you stop thinking of it as something extra.

The Prim may look like a beautiful pillow.

But the seven inches it adds to your bed could reveal sensations you did not know you were missing.
